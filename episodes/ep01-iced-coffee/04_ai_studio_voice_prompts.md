## 1. How to Use the Google AI Studio Speech Playground (3 Simple Boxes)

In your Google AI Studio **Playground** screen:
1. **Box 1 (`Speaker 1` Dropdown — Top Left):**
   - Pick **one single voice** and keep that **exact same voice** for all 8 shots (for example: click the Speaker dropdown → choose **`Kore`**, **`Charon`**, **`Cruz`**, or click **Create/Design a voice** once with: *"A warm, articulate Cambodian educator and storyteller in his early 30s with a clear, resonant mid-baritone timbre, speaking at a steady, engaging pace (~130 wpm) with friendly warmth"*).
2. **Box 2 (`Style` Dropdown/Input — Top Middle, where it currently says `Hushed, terrifie...`):**
   - Click that middle dropdown and change it from `Hushed, terrified` to **`Warm, clear educational storytelling`** (or paste the `Style Box` line given for each shot below).
3. **Box 3 (`Speech Text Box` — Main Box):**
   - Delete the sample English text (`<whispers> Keep your voice down!...`), **copy-paste ONLY the pure Khmer text box below**, and click **Run** (bottom-right / `Ctrl + Enter`), then download the `.wav` file!
