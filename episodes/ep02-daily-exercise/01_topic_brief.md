## 1. Review of the director's current brief

**What is good (keep):** simple Khmer, everyday metaphors (rusty motorbike, phone battery, sponge), anthology cast, Hook & Catch bridges, $0 starting options.

**What is weak (fix):**
1. **Made-up precision.** Many hard numbers (−90%, +25%, −40%, +30%, −40–50%, $500–$1,500) have no source. For a health video, wrong numbers hurt trust and can mislead viewers.
2. **Wrong dose claim.** "30 min/day = −35% disease risk" is not what evidence says (see §2).
3. **Storyteller-only structure.** Every shot is explain + hook; no problem scenes, no dialogue (addressed in 03b).
4. **Metaphor shown as measurement.** "Battery 20% → 95%" is a drawing, but reads like a measured result.
5. **No safety line.** Missing "start gently / see a doctor if chest pain, dizziness, or you have a health condition".
6. **Shot 07 location says `gym`** while the message is "$0, no gym" — contradicts itself (use living room / street).

## 2. Fact-check table (web research, Nov 2026)

Sources: WHO Guidelines on Physical Activity & Sedentary Behaviour (2020); AHA / large cohort meta-analyses on all-cause & CVD mortality; Sports Medicine meta-analyses on post-meal walking and breaking up sitting (Engeroff et al.; Buffey et al. 2022); meta-analyses on exercise & sleep, acute exercise & cognition, exercise & depression/anxiety (umbrella reviews).
