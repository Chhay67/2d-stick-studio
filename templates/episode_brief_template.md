## 3. Deep Multi-Chapter Narrative Structure (Complete Meaning)
1. **Chapter 1 — Everyday Hook & Relatable Struggle:** Start with `STORY` and `HYBRID` shots showing a real everyday Cambodian situation in rich detail.
2. **Chapter 2 — Inside the Trap (Root Cause & Quantitative Anatomy):** Dissect the hidden numbers and root causes (`EXPLAINER` + `HYBRID`).
3. **Chapter 3 — Step-by-Step Mechanism (How It Works Inside the Body / Mind / Wallet):** Trace the chain reaction chronologically.
4. **Chapter 4 — Hidden Ripple Effects (Sleep, Stress, Hormones, or Secondary Costs):** Reveal the unseen vicious cycles.
5. **Chapter 5 — Long-Term Compounding (`30 Days → 1 Year → 5 Years`):** Show where the daily habit leads if left unchecked.
6. **Chapter 6 — The Smart Turnaround (Real-Life Application):** Follow the protagonist applying a realistic, sustainable shift.
7. **Chapter 7 — Actionable Protocol & Closing Reflection:** Clear step-by-step system viewers can practice immediately.
